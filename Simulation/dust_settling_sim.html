What is being solved

∂ρd/∂t + ∂(ρd u)/∂z = α ∂/∂z [ ρg ∂(ρd/ρg)/∂z ]
u = −z · St₀ · ez²/2, ρg = e−z²/2

Heights are in gas scale heights H and times in 1/Ω. Dust starts mixed with the gas at ρd = 0.01 ρg. St₀ is the midplane Stokes number from the Epstein stopping time, set by R and a.

How it matches the code

The solver here is a JavaScript port of the corrected dust_functions.py and diffusion_functions.py: a first-order upwind finite-volume step on cell centres, with zero flux through the midplane and the top at z = 3H, then the explicit diffusion term with zero-flux boundaries. The time step is the smaller of the advection and diffusion limits, both with a safety factor of 0.4. Dust mass is conserved to rounding error.

The dotted lines are exact results to compare against: with turbulence, the steady state ρd/ρg ∝ exp[−(St₀/α)(ez²/2 − 1)]; without it, a midplane ratio of 0.01·exp(St₀·t̂). A layer thinner than one grid cell cannot be resolved, so raise N when the solver falls short of the dotted line.

The dust band and both charts come from the grid solution. The bright grains are an illustration layer: each one follows the same settling velocity plus a random walk matched to α.
